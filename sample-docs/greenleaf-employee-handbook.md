# Greenleaf Co. Employee Handbook (sample document)

This is a made-up handbook for practising with the RAG Starter Kit. Upload it, then try the questions at the bottom.

## Working hours

Core working hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees choose when to work, as long as they complete 40 hours a week. Employees may work from home up to three days a week after their first month.

## Leave

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year; any extra unused days expire on 31 March.

Sick leave is 12 days per year. A doctor's note is needed for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver, both fully paid.

## Expenses

Travel must be booked through the TravelDesk portal. Economy class is used for flights under 6 hours. Meals during travel are reimbursed up to 1,500 rupees per day. Submit receipts within 30 days of returning, through the Expenses app.

## Equipment

New employees receive a laptop on day one. A home-office allowance of 10,000 rupees is paid once, after the first month. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT Support within 24 hours.

## IT security

Use a password manager and turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) for every work account. Never share passwords, even with IT Support. Report suspicious emails with the "Report phishing" button.

## Learning

Each employee has a yearly learning budget of 30,000 rupees for courses, books and conferences. Requests above 10,000 rupees need manager approval.
