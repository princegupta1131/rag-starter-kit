STEP 0 · THE IDEA

An open-book exam for the AI

Without RAG the AI answers from memory, like a closed-book exam: fluent, but it may invent your leave policy. With RAG it first finds the right pages of your documents, then answers only from those pages and cites them. If the pages don't contain the answer, it says so.

1. UnderstandCheck the question, reuse a cached answer, make it standalone.
2. Turn into numbersThe question becomes 768 numbers in the same meaning space as the chunks.
3. FindSearch by meaning and by keywords, then fuse the two lists.
4. DecideIs the best match good enough? Keep only the closely related chunks.
5. Answer + checkThe AI answers from numbered sources; citations are verified.
STEP 1 · GUARD

Cheap checks before spending any AI time

Plain rules, no AI call, so it takes under a millisecond. It stops empty questions, very long inputs, and obvious attempts to hijack the AI ("ignore your instructions…").

Why first: every later step costs time or money. A bad input stopped here costs nothing.

    STEP 2 · CACHE

    Asked before? Answer instantly

    The kit keeps recent answers in memory for 10 minutes. The key is the question in lower case plus a fingerprint of the documents, so when anyone uploads or deletes a document the old answers stop matching automatically.

    • Follow-up questions ("until when?") are never cached: they depend on the conversation.
    • One server keeps its own cache; for several servers you'd use Redis.

    STEP 3 · REWRITE

    Make the question searchable on its own

    People ask follow-ups like "until when?". Those words alone match nothing. A small AI call rewrites the question into a standalone search query using the conversation, and fixes typos.

    Only for searching: the answer step still sees the original question. The kit also searches the original question next to the rewrite, so a bad rewrite can't hide the right chunk.

    STEP 4 · EMBED THE QUESTION

    The question goes into the same meaning space

    The same embedding model that turned every chunk into 768 numbers now turns the question into 768 numbers. Because both live in the same space, "close to the question" now means "about the same thing".

    Query vs document: the model is told which text is a question and which is a document. Questions and their answers are phrased differently ("How many days…?" vs "Every employee receives 24 days…"), and this lets the model place a question near the text that answers it.

      Meaning search and keyword search, side by side

        STEP 6 · FUSE (RECIPROCAL RANK FUSION)

        Merge the two lists by rank, not by score

        Cosine scores (0.0 to 1.0) and keyword scores (0.1, 0.3…) are on different scales, so they can't simply be added. Reciprocal Rank Fusion ignores the scores and uses positions: each list gives a chunk 1 / (60 + rank), and the chunk's total decides the order.

        • Found high in both lists → strongest.
        • The 60 softens the gap between rank 1 and rank 2, so one list can't dominate.
        • The kit keeps the best 6, and always keeps the 2 closest by meaning.

        STEP 7 · EVIDENCE GATE

        Is the best match good enough to answer from?

        If even the closest chunk is not similar enough (MIN_SIMILARITY, default 0.50), the kit stops and says it couldn't find this. No AI call, no chance to invent.

        If it passes, only chunks within 0.12 of the best (or found by keyword) go to the AI, so loosely related text doesn't distract it.

        Similar is not the same as answered. "What is the company's stock price?" passes the gate (its best match is about a company budget). The second safety net, the AI's instruction to reply NOT_FOUND when the sources don't contain the answer, catches it in step 8.

          STEP 8 · ANSWER

          The AI writes from the numbered sources only

          The chat model receives strict rules, the numbered sources and the question. It must cite every fact like [1], use no outside knowledge, answer in the question's language, and reply exactly NOT_FOUND when the sources don't hold the answer.

          Safety net 1
          The gate (step 7): no good match, no AI call.
          Safety net 2
          The prompt: no answer in the sources → NOT_FOUND.
