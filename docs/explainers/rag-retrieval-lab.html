    Cache

    Ask it twice

    Ten people ask the same question. Should the AI do all the work ten times?

    Ask, ask again, then upload a new document and ask once more.Example times
    0.0 s
      Ask, ask again, then upload a document and ask again to unlock this discovery.

      Why?

      Ten people asking "how many leave days?" shouldn't cost ten AI calls.

      The science, simply

      The saved answer is labelled with the question plus a fingerprint of the documents. Upload or delete a file and the fingerprint changes, so old answers are never reused by mistake.

      In the kit

      step2_cache.py. CACHE_TTL_SECONDS=600: answers are kept for 10 minutes.
      Go deeper: what is not cached

      Follow-up questions ("until when?") are never cached: their meaning depends on the conversation. The cache lives in the server's memory, so with several servers you'd move it to Redis.

            Why?

            Meaning handles different words and languages. Exact words catch what meaning can blur: product codes, names, acronyms like "2FA".

            The science, simply

            Meaning: the closest arrows (cosine, Lab 1). Words: Postgres reduces words to roots ("carried" → carri) and finds cards that contain them.

            In the kit

            step5_retrieve.py and the SQL in repositories/chunks.py. Each search returns up to 20 cards.
            Go deeper: about this demo

            To make the ranking visible, this page treats each handbook sentence as a small card (12 cards). In the kit the handbook is 2 cards of about 200 words, so its real scores are a little lower (around 0.57–0.65), because each card mixes several topics.

            06
            Rank fusion (RRF)

            The podium

            One judge scores 0.80, the other 0.3. You can't add those. So how do you pick a winner?

            Award the points. Each list gives 1/(60 + place): 1st = 1/61, 2nd = 1/62…Real ranks
              Award the points to unlock this discovery.

              Why?

              The two searches score on different scales, so their scores can't be added. Their places can.

              The science, simply

              Like two judges ranking dancers by place. The 60 keeps 1st and 2nd place close, so one list can't overpower the other.

              In the kit

              step5_retrieve.py: RRF_K = 60, TOP_K=6 in .env. The 2 closest by meaning are always kept.

              07
              Evidence gate

              Clear the bar

              If nothing in the handbook is close enough, should the AI still answer? What could go wrong?

              Drag the bar up or down, then make the best match jump.Real scores
              Make the best match jump to unlock this discovery.

              Why?

              Without it, the AI would get loosely related text and might stitch together a confident wrong answer.

              The science, simply

              Below the bar, the question stops with "couldn't find this" and no AI call. But similar isn't the same as answered: a card can be close in topic and still not contain the answer. Step 8 catches that.

              In the kit

              step6_answer.py. MIN_SIMILARITY=0.50, CONTEXT_MARGIN=0.12.

                The open-book exam

                The AI knows a lot about the world. Why don't we let it use what it knows?

                Hand the AI its sources and watch it write.Example answer
                What the AI gets
                • Use only these sources, no outside knowledge

                • Reply in the question's language
                • No answer in the sources → reply NOT_FOUND
                  What the AI writes
                  Let the AI write to unlock this discovery.

                  Why?

                  An open-book exam: the AI may use only the pages it was given, and must say which page. That's what makes the answer trustworthy.

                  The science, simply

                  Language models follow the instructions in the prompt. Strict rules + only relevant text + "say NOT_FOUND if it isn't there" = the second safety net, after the gate.

                  In the kit

                  The rules: prompts/answer_system.md (edit them to change tone or language). The call: step6_answer.py.
